◷Clock

Connect to your hub to read the clock.

Tap anywhere to move through the menu. Short power press opens/selects; double press returns home. Hold 1.5 seconds for Back, 3 seconds for Home, 4.5–7 seconds to cancel, or at least 7 seconds then release to suspend. Bedside mode uses brightness 5.

◴Watch faces

Your original designs, with editable artwork, colors and layout.

Edit selected face

Digit rectangles use x,y,width,height on a 320 × 240 canvas. Colors are six hexadecimal digits. Save changes to the device; Reset restores the supplied design. No sidebar is included.

◐Appearance

Customize the menus and information area. Keep a face’s original colors, or recolor it with your theme.

To switch later, enter its name in the Clock color field and apply. Bedside mode dims the selected colors. Saved themes survive updates.

◷Timers & visual alarm

Alarms are visual and silent in this release. Keep the device powered and awake.

Device features

Weather

Unusual weather thresholds

Thunderstorms, heavy rain, freezing rain and heavy snow also count. These are modeled conditions, not official emergency alerts.

Initial location: Johannesburg; change coordinates to your location. Data from Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0. Weather refreshes every 15 minutes while the clock is visible. Disabled weather has no worker or network requests.

Notification forwarding

Send from the paired Windows app using fracture notify APP TITLE BODY, or POST app, title and body to /api/notifications with your hub token. Notifications are silent. Automatic Windows toast capture requires a packaged, consented listener and is not included in this build.

♫Windows media

Connect the Spotify desktop app through Windows media sessions. No Spotify developer account, client ID or Spotify login is needed.

In the native app: Connection → Get sensor modules, then Check sensor. If access is denied, choose Authorize sensor in the native app. Supports Intel and AMD Zen on x64; requires the official signed driver. The PC temperature is labeled PC on the clock and takes priority while its worker is active.

Uses LRCLIB, or lyrics supplied to the authenticated lyrics webhook. Lookups send song title, artist, album and duration. Lyrics availability and timing vary by track.

Keep the Windows tray app running and open Spotify. The media worker starts only while its player or an enabled status-bar widget is visible. Track details and artwork travel through your paired hub. Play/pause and previous/next target Spotify; volume controls the Windows default audio output, affecting other apps too.

Requires Windows 10 1809 or later and a Spotify app that publishes a Windows media session. Lyrics can show synchronized or plain text through the hub. YouTube support and TomTom speaker streaming are not implemented. Windows runtime verification is still pending.

⌘Hub connection & privacy

Use the connection details shown by fracture pair-info on your Linux hub. Keep its token private.

Linux hub settings

Disk shows used space. Traffic shows the larger of download / upload in KiB/s, excluding duplicate container/USB traffic unless that interface is selected.

The metrics switch affects the hub when changed on that machine. Windows metrics collection is not included in 0.1.9.
