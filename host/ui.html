Theme colors

Wallpaper & background

Sync your PC desktop wallpaper automatically, pick a solid colour (default legacy black), choose a preset, or crop a custom image. Menus use escalating frosted glass blur so text stays readable.

Windows automatically forwards your current desktop wallpaper when paired or updated.

Weather

Current conditions and forecast are supplied by Open-Meteo; the TomTom does not make internet requests.

Unusual weather thresholds

Thunderstorms, heavy/freezing rain and heavy snow also count. Conditions are modeled estimates, not official emergency alerts.

Data from Open-Meteo under CC BY 4.0.

Today agenda

A private, read-only calendar summary. Nothing is fetched until the TomTom Today page is open.

The URL is saved only in the host's local settings and is not included in logs.

Notifications

Forwarding is opt-in. The app keeps no notification history; new text and app logos go to your paired host and TomTom.

Windows media and lyrics

Share media-session details from the Windows PC without Spotify account credentials.

TomTom audio

System-output capture is disabled until enabled. A microphone is never used.

Connection mode and pairing

Plug your TomTom directly into this PC over USB or route through a Linux hub on your LAN.

Connects over HTTPS to your Linux hub (`fracture pair-info`).

Advanced host network settings

In Direct USB mode on Windows, saving automatically provisions local TLS certificates and connects to 192.168.101.115.

Updates

Checks GitHub, verifies downloaded checksums and preserves your settings.
